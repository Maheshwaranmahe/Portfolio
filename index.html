About me

MA Mehzabin Afroz
✦

Hello! I'm Mehzabin Afroz, a creative and detail-oriented Graphic Designer from Dhaka, Bangladesh. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology.

I have been working as a Freelance Graphic Designer since 2021, with experience across logo design, brand identity, typography design, and social media visuals.

"Good design isn't just how it looks — it's how it communicates and works."
